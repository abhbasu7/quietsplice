# Chrome Web Store submission

1. Build the ZIP with `python scripts/package_release.py`.
2. Test a skippable YouTube ad and an HBO ad break. Check that audio returns at the end, already-muted videos stay muted, and the badge works in fullscreen. Test HBO Skip Intro separately.
3. Upload the ZIP and use the text and links in [the store listing](LISTING.md). Add the icon, promo tile, and demo screenshot.
4. Enter the privacy and permission details from [the privacy policy](../PRIVACY.md) and [reviewer notes](REVIEWER-NOTES.md).
5. Choose public visibility and submit for review.
